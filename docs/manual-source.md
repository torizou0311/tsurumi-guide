## 2. Access
- Clicking the address opens Google Maps.
- From Haneda Airport by train to "Keikyu-Tsurumi Station": about 15–20 minutes (sources disagree 15 vs 20). Keikyu-Tsurumi Station → accommodation: about 18 min on foot.
- From Narita Airport by train to "Tsurumi Station (JR Line)": about 90 minutes. JR Tsurumi Station → accommodation: about 12 min on foot.
- By taxi: give the driver the address 横浜市鶴見区寺谷1-7-40 and show the map. From Haneda: about 30 min, about 6,000–7,000 yen. From JR Tsurumi Station: about 3 min, about 500 yen. Taxis between 10:00 PM and 5:00 AM have a 20% late-night surcharge.
  IMPORTANT: Ask the driver to turn right at the red-circled spot on the map. If the driver goes straight down the main road instead, you will have to walk up stairs to reach the building.
- Taxi stand at Keikyu Tsurumi Station: go to the West Exit, turn left and go down the stairs, you will see the "TAXI" sign.
- Taxi stand at JR Tsurumi Station: cross the crosswalk at the bottom of the stairs and turn left, you will see the "TAXI" sign.

## 10. Troubleshooting
- Entrance keypad failure: there is a box next to the entrance containing the key. If the keypad isn't working, contact us via Airbnb chat; we'll send the box code.
- No hot water (shower room): press the power button at the upper left of the hot water panel. If the green light on the power button is on, hot water is supplied even if the numbers are off.
- Power outage: breaker is at the entrance. Open the cover and lift the bar up.
- Lost and found: message us on the reservation site. Cleaning staff will check; if found, we send it cash on delivery. If no message within 3 days after checkout, items are disposed of without notice. Inexpensive items (umbrellas, towels, etc.) are disposed of without notice.
